What leaves my computer.

One file: the sealed list, and a handful of totals. The list is built from an allow-list, so a field that is not named there cannot be published by mistake.

In the sealed list

  • How many courses, and how many the university has locked
  • For each course: its term, counts of items, graded work, documents and announcements, the total size, the first and last month
  • For each document: its kind (pdf, docx, pptx), its size in KB, whether it was saved
  • For each run: the day, the Agent's steps, the cost, how many rows were new
  • A fingerprint per course and per document, made with a secret salt that never leaves my computer

Never in it

  • A course name or code, a document name, an announcement
  • A score, a grade or any feedback
  • A person's name: mine, a teacher's or a classmate's
  • The portal's ids, and TinyFish's run ids
  • The length of a name: the bars on each door take their length from the fingerprint, because a real length could give a course away to anyone holding the catalogue

A test builds the sealed list and this site from an export and fails if any word of that export appears in them. You can read the list this site was built from: sealed.json.

My sign-in

  • I type my password once, into Blackboard's own page, inside a TinyFish browser. It is never in this code, in a settings file or in an Agent goal.
  • The session's cookies stay in TinyFish's browser. They are not copied to my computer.
  • I do not use the password vault: it would store my university password with a third party.
  • When I am done I delete the profile on TinyFish, and the saved session is gone.

Other people, and TinyFish

  • Only my own account, and only reading.
  • Classmates' names and posts are not exported. A conversation is kept as its date and its number of messages.
  • TinyFish sees what its Agent and its browser see, and Fetch reads each PDF and Word file to return its text. That is the service doing its work; nothing goes anywhere else.
  • TinyFish's run pages hold real pictures of my portal, so their links go to the judges only.
